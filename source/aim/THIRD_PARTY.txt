Input note
Horizontal FOV is 103 degrees. Yaw/pitch delta = mouse movement × VAL Sens ×
0.07 degrees. Keep the same physical mouse DPI as in VALORANT.
Raw mouse input is requested where supported; a browser fallback may be affected
by operating-system pointer acceleration and is not guaranteed to feel identical.
